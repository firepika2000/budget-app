# v0.11 Mobile Speed / Apple integrations closure audit

Status: **ENGINEERING CANDIDATE — focused verification complete; human acceptance remains required**

## Implemented scope

ClearPocket's Apple integrations accelerate navigation and entry without creating a second financial
system or exposing budget data outside the authenticated app:

- Siri and Shortcuts actions open Home, Plan, Activity, Accounts, Insights, and Household through the
  existing active-budget production shell;
- Add ClearPocket Transaction accepts optional payee, exact currency text, memo, a non-future date,
  and expense/income type, then opens the canonical transaction editor for review and explicit Save;
- quick-entry drafts are length-bounded, one-shot, and expire after five minutes;
- future shortcut dates fail with guidance to use Schedule Transaction rather than posting future money;
- the small and medium Home Screen widget displays no financial or household data and launches only
  exact, allowlisted ClearPocket routes;
- widget URLs cannot carry payee, amount, memo, credentials, identifiers, or other private query data;
- optional per-budget reminders cover at most 50 active scheduled items in the next 60 days and use a
  generic lock-screen message with no payee, amount, account, category, or member detail;
- every shortcut and widget destination still passes through normal authentication, active-budget
  resolution, capabilities, repository selection, validation, and canonical application services.

No App Intent, widget, or notification extension reads money or mutates financial state. Rich financial
widgets remain intentionally out of scope until they have an explicit disclosure, authorization,
shared-container, and redaction contract.

## Consolidated human acceptance

Do not reset retained app or server data.

1. In Shortcuts, run **Add ClearPocket Transaction** with payee, amount, memo, a past date, and type.
   Confirm the existing transaction editor opens with those values and nothing is saved until Save.
2. Run it with a future date. Confirm ClearPocket directs you to Schedule Transaction and does not
   create or prefill a future posted transaction.
3. Add the small and medium ClearPocket widgets. Confirm Add Transaction, Plan, Accounts, and Insights
   open the authenticated active budget and no private budget data appears on the Home Screen.
4. Enable scheduled reminders for a disposable active schedule. Confirm the notification is generic,
   opens the app normally, and disabling reminders removes ClearPocket-owned pending requests.
5. Repeat a shortcut while signed out or without access to the previously active budget. Confirm the
   normal authentication/chooser boundary appears and no hidden budget information is revealed.

Human evidence must be recorded separately; this audit does not claim human acceptance.
