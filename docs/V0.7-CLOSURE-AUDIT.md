## Delivered scope

- Actionable Home with capability-scoped canonical quick actions, ranked attention, upcoming context,
  recent activity, and intentional empty states.
- Personal server-persisted category favorites and focused Plan modes without changing money.
- Persistent per-member/per-budget Hide Amounts, masked accessibility values, and app-switcher shielding.
- Human-readable View Only, Limited Access, Full Access, and Custom household policies with scoped
  resources, optimistic edits, attribution, and non-disclosing authorization.
- Recoverable invitation/member lifecycle with single-use tokens, removal/reactivation, protected
  ownership, and durable audit events.
- Complete delegated request lifecycle and owner allowance management, with exact-money issuance and
  PostgreSQL contention protection.
- Optional seven-part guided onboarding that explains consequences and routes into real production
  workflows without mutating financial state.

## Privacy and authorization closure

The focused security matrix passed across budget discovery, resource-scoped accounts/categories,
transaction search and total counts, report aggregates and filters, payee search/aliases, attachments,
targets, allowances, delegated actions, and ID-addressed mutations. Hidden resources do not contribute
selectors, counts, aggregates, suggestions, distinguishable errors, or mutable targets. Owner-only
member lifecycle and access-policy routes remain owner-only; Full Access does not transfer ownership.

## Human acceptance still required

Do not repeat already accepted v0.5/v0.6 behavior. The smallest meaningful v0.7 pass is:

1. **Daily workspace:** confirm Home actions open the real editors, attention opens category
   resolution, Plan favorites/focus are usable, and Hide Amounts remains masked through background /
   foreground and relaunch.
2. **Household administration:** invite one test member, assign Limited/Custom access, verify the
   member sees only selected resources, remove and re-invite the same member, and confirm prior access
   recovery plus the owner audit trail.
3. **Delegated lifecycle:** as the delegated member, submit then cancel one request and submit a second;
   as owner request changes then approve it, create/issue/pause/reactivate one allowance, and verify
   only approval/issuance moves exact allocation money.
4. **Fresh-budget education:** create or use a genuinely empty owner budget, advance and skip the guide,
   resume from Profile, route into one real tab, then restart after completion. Confirm a populated or
   delegated budget is never interrupted.
5. **Accessibility spot check:** repeat the guide and household editor at one accessibility text size
   and with VoiceOver for headings, consequences, controls, masked amounts, and focus order.
