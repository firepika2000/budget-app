# v0.9 Planning Power closure audit

Status: **ENGINEERING CANDIDATE — focused verification and human acceptance remain required**

This audit follows the superseding release sequence at the top of `ROADMAP.md`. In that sequence,
v0.9 is the Planning Power milestone. The older v0.9 Distribution, Installation & Server Manager
section remains a required cross-cutting workstream through v1.0; it does not replace this milestone.

## Implemented scope

The production Plan now uses the canonical dated allocation ledger and exact target guidance across
Live Server, Local Device, and deterministic Demo providers. The implemented milestone includes:

- monthly funding, refill-balance, by-date, recurring-expense, and calendar-aware weekly targets;
- recurring cadence anchored without month-end drift, minimum contributions, target priority, and
  selected-month effective due dates;
- month-specific target snooze/resume without changing the target's global active state;
- focused Plan views for all, favorites, underfunded, overspent, funded, and available categories;
- per-group suggested funding and trailing average spending, with qualified category identity;
- checked, exact Monthly plan cost presentation that excludes snoozed targets and does not claim to
  forecast every expense;
- Smart Funding preview, shortfall explanation, priority ordering, explicit confirmation, optimistic
  versioning, delegated authorization, and all-date real-cash limits;
- current and future-month assignments of existing money, with future income remaining unavailable;
- positive carry and prospective cash-overspending policy history with owner controls and provenance;
- first-class allocation/move history and persisted future-month Local Device planning;
- overflow, calendar-boundary, privacy, reload, migration, recovery, and provider-parity regressions.

Suggestions, targets, schedules, forecasts, and scenarios remain non-mutating until an authorized
user explicitly commits through the canonical allocation or transaction service. No Planning Power
feature changes account balances, creates income, or makes forecast money spendable.

## Consolidated human acceptance flow

Use one disposable budget or categories; no reset of retained household data is required.

1. Open Plan, move one month forward, assign existing cash, return to the current month, then revisit
   the future month. Confirm each month's assignment is independent and survives relaunch.
2. Create or edit a recurring target, verify the displayed due occurrence and recommendation, snooze
   only the selected month, then confirm the following month resumes automatically.
3. Compare All, Favorites, Underfunded, Overspent, Funded, and Available focus views. Confirm the
   chosen focus persists for that budget and hidden/restricted categories never appear.
4. Confirm each group shows a sensible Suggested amount and Average spent value. Open the group and
   verify its categories remain qualified and actionable.
5. Open Smart Funding, verify the preview explains any remaining shortfall, cancel once without
   mutation, then confirm once. Reopen and confirm already-funded amounts are not proposed again.
6. Review Monthly plan cost wording and the allocation history. Confirm account balances and posted
   activity did not change merely from viewing guidance, snoozing, focusing, or cancelling previews.

## Explicitly outside this closure

- A dedicated authoritative debt-payoff target remains separate from the implemented read-only saved
  payoff scenario and should not be improvised as a category target without an account-level contract.
- Broader server-mode offline mutation conflict resolution remains future work; transaction creation
  already has an idempotent queue and Local Device is fully offline-authoritative.
- Windows/QNAP signing, Windows hardware acceptance, and end-to-end nontechnical server onboarding
  remain distribution gates through v1.0.
- TestFlight publication, merging, and tagging require an explicit later release decision.
